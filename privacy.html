Grapleton privacy

Last updated 24 November 2026.

Your cellar

Grapleton saves rack names, wine details and attached photos locally on your device. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics or server upload for your collection. The web app stores this information in your browser profile. The Android app stores it in its own app storage. These are separate collections.

Photos

When you choose Take photo or Choose photo, Grapleton opens the device camera or photo picker. Only the selected photo is attached to your bottle. Images are resized and re-encoded before they are saved. The Android app does not request access to your entire photo library or save copies to your gallery.

External services

Choosing Look up wine opens Google Search with the bottle name and vintage. Google receives that search and handles it under its own privacy policy. Visiting the web prototype also makes normal requests to its GitHub Pages hosting provider. Android includes the app files and can work without contacting that website.

Retention and deletion

Saved data stays until you remove the bottle or clear the app or browser storage. Uninstalling the Android app removes its local cellar. You can export an unencrypted backup file, including photos, and restore it through Settings. Keep exported files private. Choosing a destination in the Android share sheet shares that backup with the selected service. This release has no automatic sync. Routine app updates preserve its storage. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for the Android package.

Contact

For privacy questions about the prototype, contact the project maintainer through the Grapleton issue tracker. Issues are public, so keep your cellar details and photos private when reporting a problem.
